Built for planning engineers who review programmes

Open an IFC model and a schedule. 4D Sequencer links the model elements to the activities automatically.

Finds impossible sequences

Reads the model to find work that starts before the element it stands on, sits in or hangs from is finished: columns before the slab under them, doors before the wall around them, ceilings before the slab above. Each finding is shown on the model.

Checks the baseline

12 standard quality checks: missing links, negative and positive lags, Start-to-Finish links, hard constraints, high and negative float, long durations, invalid dates and out-of-sequence progress. Export them as numbered review comments in Excel, ready to send back to the contractor.

Plays the schedule day by day

Watch the project being built with the Gantt chart moving in step. Works with Primavera P6 (XER and XML), Microsoft Project XML, Excel and CSV schedules.

Shows delays on the model

Baseline against actual, with late work in red and early work in green, or both side by side in split view.

Compares revisions

Load an earlier revision or update and see what slipped, what improved, what was added or deleted, and which links changed.

Reports in one click

PowerPoint, Excel, an interactive web report and an MP4 video of the sequence.

Cost and cash flow

Budget, spent to date and forecast at completion from Primavera P6 resource costs.

Your files stay with you

No account, no sign-in, no tracking. Everything runs on your computer and works offline.

Which files can I open?

Models in IFC format. Schedules from Primavera P6 (XER or XML), Microsoft Project (XML), Excel or CSV.

Does it need the internet?

No. 4D Sequencer runs on your computer and does not upload your files anywhere. Activation also works offline.

What does it need to run?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) with Microsoft Edge, which is part of Windows, or Google Chrome.
